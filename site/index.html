01 About

Building things that ship — from the model to the app store.

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at Keshav Memorial Engineering College, Hyderabad, building AI-powered products that reach real users.

I design and ship mobile apps, web platforms, and AI systems — FIZI is on the Google Play Store, SplitPlan is live with real users, and my computer vision work spans custom CNNs engineered from mathematical first principles. I care deeply about the full loop: user research, interactive design systems, model development, and rock-solid deployment.

Flutter and Python are where most of my engineering lives. I’m fascinated by the intersection of machine learning and product design — building clean, responsive interfaces that make complex intelligent models feel effortless and trustworthy.

  1. i.

    Ship to real users

    A model that lives only in a notebook doesn’t solve problems. FIZI is on Google Play. SplitPlan is live. I build software for real human workflows.

  2. ii.

    Build the feedback loop

    The best systems observe themselves and improve — from Operation Evolve’s self-improving training pipelines to Collaborative AI’s multi-model consensus deliberation.

  3. iii.

    Design is as critical as the model

    An intelligent algorithm is only as effective as the interface that exposes it. I design the design tokens, interactions, and component libraries alongside the model.
